# Fintasy: Battle-Royale Stock Market Simulator (Stock Royale)

## Core Vision
Level up Fintasy into a full-scale battle-royale inspired trading simulator. 30 to 60 players drop into market sectors, loot tickers, engage in 30-second high-stakes trading duels, manage circle collapses/storm tick damage, third-party active fights, and fight down to the final circle for the #1 Victory Royale!

## Key Pillars
1. **Lobby & Matchmaking**: 30-60 player matches with instant AI bot fill for quick matching.
2. **The Sector Map**: S&P 500 Market Treemap topology (Outer defensive sectors to Inner AI/Big-Tech hot sectors).
3. **The Storm**: Federal Reserve / Market Liquidity drain storm shrinking sectors inward; players caught in the storm suffer dollar tick damage to their starting pot ($15,000).
4. **Trading Duels & Looting**: Contested drops or sector rotations trigger 30-60 second micro-trading duels. Winner keeps net profit and loots opponent tickers/capital; loser is eliminated or forced to retreat.
5. **Third-Partying**: Roaming traders can see active battles on radar and jump in, turning duels into 3-way or 4-way chaotic trading brawls.
6. **Football Manager (FM) Tactical UI**: Tactical radar heatmap, live order book, split-screen duel HUD, kill feed, and ticker tape.
7. **Competitive Ranked System**: Rocket League style ranking (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Champion, Golden Trader) with Elo/MMR tracking and seasonal leaderboards.
