  • [MATCH]

    Lobbies that always fill

    30–60 traders per match. Empty seats go to AI bots playing Scalper, Swing or Degenerate risk styles.

  • [MAP]

    Eleven sectors, three rings

    Utilities and real estate on the outside, AI semiconductors and high-beta at the core. The storm closes them over 5 rounds.

  • [DUEL]

    The fight is a trade

    Contested sectors trigger 30–60 second trading duels: long or short, 1x–5x leverage. The winner takes the P&L and the loot.

  • [CHAOS]

    Third parties welcome

    Anyone nearby can join a live duel. Multi-way P&L decides who walks away with what.

  • [ENGINE]

    Every match replays exactly

    Prices tick at 10 Hz from seeded Geometric Brownian Motion with sector correlation and event shocks.

  • [RANKED]

    Copper to Golden Trader

    MMR from placement, liquidations and lobby strength, with divisions I–III in every tier.
