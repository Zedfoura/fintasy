# Scratchpad — Fintasy Stock Royale

## Operational Notes
- Starting capital: $15,000 virtual cash per participant.
- Lobby capacity: 30-60 players. Auto-fill with bot profiles (e.g. ScalperBot, DegenerateHodler, ValueInvestAI) to ensure zero queue wait.
- Map sectors modeled after visual market heatmap:
  - Outer: Utilities ($NEE, $DUK), Real Estate ($AMT, $PLD), Basic Materials ($LIN, $APD), Construction ($CAT, $DE).
  - Mid: Healthcare ($UNH, $JNJ), Financials ($JPM, $BRK-B), Energy ($XOM, $CVX), Consumer Cyclical ($AMZN, $TSLA).
  - Inner / Hot Zone: Big Tech Software ($MSFT, $GOOGL), AI / Semiconductors ($NVDA, $AMD, $TSM), High-Beta Meme Basket.
- Circle Collapse: 5 rounds of storm shrinking. Outside safe zone = $50-$200/sec tick damage to cash pot.
- Dueling: 30-second rapid market simulation. Traders enter Long/Short positions with instant orders. Highest % gain/dollar profit at buzzer wins the contested ticker loot and opponent bounty.
- Third-Partying: Nearby traders can jump into an ongoing duel, creating a 3-way split terminal.
- Rank Tiers: Bronze (I-III), Silver (I-III), Gold (I-III), Platinum (I-III), Diamond (I-III), Champion (I-III), Golden Trader.
